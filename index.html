01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A scholar’s curiosity.
A teacher’s heart.

I am a literature scholar with interests in Indian Writing in English, Partition trauma, gender and sexuality, translation, and cultural studies. My academic work brings literary texts into conversation with history, identity, language and society.

Alongside research, I teach English Literature, English Language and Communicative English. I aim to make learning thoughtful, approachable and alive to the world beyond the classroom.

This profile brings together my teaching, academic interests and selected work in one place.
